# 14-Day Japan Autumn Express Holiday Itinerary (October 2026)

## 0. Executive Summary

- **Duration:** 14 Days / 13 Nights (Oct 10 – Oct 23, 2026)
- **Destination:** Japan Only (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Hakone/Fuji)
- **Flight Routing:**
  - **Inbound:** Sydney (SYD) → Tokyo Haneda (HND) | Direct Flight (Oct 10, 2026)
  - **Outbound:** Tokyo Haneda (HND) → Sydney (SYD) | Direct Flight (Oct 23, 2026)
  - **Total Flight Cost:** $4,072.72 AUD total return fare (2 travelers, $2,036.36 per person)
- **Map:** [Interactive Japan Itinerary Routing Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=14uls7QPn8ESDw8Q6V40x5HKWhSHoYiA&usp=sharing)
- **Logistics:** Non-stop direct international flights to minimize travel fatigue. Tokaido Shinkansen bullet train transit between Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka.
- **Budget Estimate:** $12,427.72 AUD total for 2 travelers (Premium Mid-Range)

> [!TIP]
> **Seasonal Weather Advantage (October in Japan):** October offers ideal travel conditions across Honshu, featuring clear sunny skies, low humidity, and pleasant daytime temperatures (15–22°C). Early autumn foliage begins in northern Honshu and high-altitude areas, while crisp air provides optimal visibility of Mount Fuji.
